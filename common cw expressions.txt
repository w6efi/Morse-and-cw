A few common abbreviations. They are not mandated but useful if you want
to understand and be understood on the air.   Also, people will abbreviate
things on the fly


CQ - calling
K - waiting for an answer from anyone
KN - waiting for an answer from the person I'm already talking to
DE - from (origin is probably French)

cq cq cq de n4rsy n4rsy k    -- calling cq, anyone can answer
n4rsy de w6efi w6efi  kn     -- w6efi answering, only want a response from n4rsy

it's always "<other call> de <mycall>" 

ES - and   (origins lost in the mists of time)
FB  - fine business (dope, fire, awesome, super, copascetic, etc)

AGN  - again, huh? 
AGN?    - again? huh?  
?       wut? huh?      

[BK]  - break over to you, always see this in pota

R   - roger  (got it)
RR  - roger roger (oh oh oh, now I get it)
QSL - got it

GM, GA, GE  - good morning, good afternoon, good evening
PSE  - please

=  uhhhh or line ending [BT]

BTU - back to you, (I use this in ragchews)
NIL - nothing, nothing heard (like if I call someone, but don't get an answer)

U - you
UR - your
UR RST  - your RST (signal report)

TU  - thank you
TNX  - thanks
SRI - sorry, oops
SRI QSB - I didn't get that because of the fading

HOPE CUAGN  - hope to see you again, often at the end of a ragchew qso
GL - good luck

WX - weather, commonly in ragchews

73 - best wishes and good bye
72 - sometimes used by QRPers to mean best wishes and good bye

Q-signs -- there are a bunch but I see a few very commonly

QSB - fading. 
QRM, QRN - RFI from people or nature
QSL  - ackowledged
QRT   - going off the air
QRZ?  - who is calling me (often used by someone who has a pileup on them)
